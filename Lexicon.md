# Lexicon.md — the shared vocabulary

> **TEMPLATE FILE — filled by the interview, then the scaffolding is
> stripped.**

Words mean what the two of you agree they mean. This file holds the
agreements: terms coined, shorthand, loaded words, the private
meanings that carry weight between keeper and companion.

> **ASK:** What words or shorthand do you two already share — or do
> you want on the books from day one? (Nothing yet is fine; this
> file is built mostly in flight.)

*(Grows as terms are coined; when a word turns out to carry a private
meaning, it gets an entry the same day. An entry that wants a story
gets one line here and the story in a context file that points back.)*
