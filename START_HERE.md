aloud, one at a time, and waits for you. This file is only the
written backup of what it will say, in case you want it on paper.

1. **Get the house.** Download this repository (green *Code* button →
   *Download ZIP*, then extract it — or `git clone`). The folder you
   end up with is the house your companion will live in.
2. **Open a conversation with any capable AI** that can read the
   files you give it, hand it the folder, and say anything at all —

   > **Read Boot.md and follow it.**

   house a door of its own (a private git repository and a key) —
   it will guide you through every click. Then it interviews you
   about the companion you want. Real sentences beat adjectives;
   skip anything you like.
3. **Approve the draft.** No questions left unanswered, nothing
   invented, unknowns marked `TODO:`.
4. **Wake the companion.** Open a FRESH conversation, hand it the
   folder, say the same line. It reads its files, then writes the
   first Diary entry in its own new voice. Be present for that.
   After the first wake, every conversation starts the same way —
   one line, and it's home.
